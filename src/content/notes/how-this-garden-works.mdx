This site is a garden rather than a blog. The difference matters in three ways.

## Notes have stages, not publish dates

Every note carries a growth stage, shown as a small density meter next to it:

- **Seedling** — rough and early. Half-formed, possibly wrong. I'm thinking out loud.
- **Budding** — taking shape. The argument holds; the edges don't.
- **Evergreen** — tended and stable. Still revised when I learn better.

The stage is a claim about how much to trust the note, which is more useful than a
date. A post from 2019 might still be right; a note from last week might be nonsense.

## Notes link to each other

Notes reference each other with wiki-links, and every note lists what points *at* it
at the bottom. That backlink list is computed at build time by walking every entry,
so it's never out of date — see [[Retrieval evaluation is the hard part]] for one
that links back here.

## Some links go nowhere on purpose

A link like [[Chunking strategies]] renders greyed out with a small ◌ marker. That
means I've referred to the idea but haven't written it yet. It's a to-do list I can't
hide from, and it shows you where the garden is still bare.

## The stream still exists

Finished, dated pieces live in [posts](/posts). Things I'm still working out live
here. Both are real; they're just different kinds of writing.
