A five-stage sequential pipeline that takes messy clinical documents and produces
FHIR-compatible structured output. Vision-heavy extraction is handled by Pixtral,
with Gemini 2.0 Flash driving the reasoning stages. Deployed on Streamlit Cloud.

## Why it's staged

Each stage has a narrow contract and can be evaluated on its own, which makes
failures attributable rather than mysterious — the same argument I make in
[[Retrieval evaluation is the hard part]].
